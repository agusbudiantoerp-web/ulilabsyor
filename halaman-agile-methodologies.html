Laboratorium Rumus

Geser slider atau ubah angka. Semua rumus ada juga di file Excel.

1. Velocity & Prakiraan Sprint

Velocity = rata² poin 3 sprint terakhir
Sprint tersisa = ⌈Backlog / Velocity⌉

2. Burndown Sprint

Ideal sisa = Total × (1 − hari/durasi)

3. Prioritas RICE (Tambahan)

Skor = Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort
Beda dari RICE Blue Ocean di PDF (Reduce-Increase-Create-Eliminate).

4. Kanban: Hukum Little

Lead time = WIP / Throughput

5. Mesin Pertumbuhan Lean Startup

k = undangan × konversi   LTV = ARPU×margin/churn

6. Kapasitas Sprint

Jam = (anggota×hari − cuti) × jam/hari × faktor fokus
