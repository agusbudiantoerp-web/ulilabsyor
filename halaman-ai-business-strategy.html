1. NPV, ROI, Payback proyek AI

NPV = Σ [ CFt / (1+r)^t ] − I0
ROI = (ΣCF − I0) / I0
Payback = I0 / CF per tahun

2. Kualitas model: Precision, Recall, F1

Precision = TP/(TP+FP)
Recall = TP/(TP+FN)
F1 = 2·P·R/(P+R)
Accuracy = (TP+TN)/Total

3. Skor prioritas use case AI

Skor = (Nilai × Kelayakan × Kesiapan Data) / Risiko
