AI Quant Fund

Research prototype · iterative development

A modular research workflow combining market-regime analysis, technical and fundamental factors, machine-learning signals, portfolio construction, and risk controls to support transparent investment research.

Quantitative research and portfolio decision-support system for the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX)

Project Context

Investors need a repeatable process to screen a broad equity universe, distinguish market regimes, evaluate risk, and compare candidate portfolios without treating a single model score as a guaranteed trading signal.

Core Capabilities

  • IDX equity-universe screening and data-quality checks
  • Market regime classification and sector-level context
  • Technical and factor signals, including momentum, trend, and volume
  • Machine-learning experiments using XGBoost and out-of-sample evaluation
  • Portfolio risk, position sizing, stress testing, and backtesting

System Architecture

Python research pipeline → market and company data preparation → feature engineering and regime analysis → factor / ML scoring → portfolio and risk engine → ranked research outputs and Excel reporting.

Technology Stack

PythonPandasXGBoostScikit-learnyfinanceExcel

Evidence & Current Scope

  • A V5/V6 research run processed 91 of 91 configured symbols and 64,837 model rows.
  • Recorded model metrics from that experiment: 56.51% accuracy and 0.5974 ROC-AUC.
  • These are historical experiment results, not evidence of future profitability or a live trading track record.

Research prototype under iterative development. Universe coverage and data availability depend on upstream sources; model performance requires ongoing out-of-sample validation.
