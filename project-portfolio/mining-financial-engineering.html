Mining Financial Engineering

Decision-support tools · multiple prototypes

A suite of mining-finance concepts and tools connecting operational assumptions with project cash flow, valuation, commodity sensitivity, and stochastic risk analysis.

Financial modeling and investment decision support for mining and heavy-equipment projects

Project Context

Mining investment decisions are exposed to uncertainty in production, recovery, costs, equipment utilization, commodity prices, and capital requirements. A decision model should make these drivers visible and test their impact on project economics.

Core Capabilities

  • Project cash-flow modeling and capital budgeting
  • NPV and IRR evaluation
  • Commodity-price, production, and cost sensitivity analysis
  • Scenario and Monte Carlo risk modeling
  • Mining tender and contract decision frameworks

System Architecture

Operational and commercial assumptions → production / cost model → project cash flow → valuation and sensitivity → stochastic risk analysis → investment or tender decision brief.

Technology Stack

PythonExcelStreamlitPandasNumPyMonte Carlo simulation

Evidence & Current Scope

  • Related work in the repository includes Mining Financial Engineering and Risk Analytics, Mining Financial Risk App, MFE Analytics, IGKM Mining Financial Decision System, and AMTIS tender-intelligence materials.
  • The portfolio groups these related prototypes under one mining-finance capability area; they are not represented as one fully integrated production platform.

Collection of prototypes, analytical pages, and decision frameworks at different maturity levels. Results depend on the quality of user-supplied operating and market assumptions.
