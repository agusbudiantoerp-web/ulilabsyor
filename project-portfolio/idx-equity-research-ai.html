AI Equity Research / Financial Markets

IDX Equity Research AI

Telegram workflow prototype · V23 iteration

A Telegram-led research assistant designed to turn a stock ticker request into a structured equity-research report, combining quantitative signals, fundamental context, market data, and analyst-style commentary.

Automated equity-research workflow for Indonesian listed companies

Project Context

Equity research often requires gathering market, financial, technical, ownership, and broker-flow information from separate sources. The project aims to organize those inputs into a repeatable report workflow.

Core Capabilities

  • Telegram command workflow for ticker-based research requests
  • Technical and fundamental research sections
  • Foreign-flow, broker-summary, sector, and market-regime context where data is available
  • AI-assisted synthesis and scoring with explicit caveats
  • Automated Excel and PDF report outputs

System Architecture

Telegram request → data collection and validation → quantitative / fundamental analysis modules → AI-assisted synthesis → report generation → Telegram delivery.

Technology Stack

PythonTelegram Bot APIGroq / Gemini integrationsPandasExcelPDF

Evidence & Current Scope

  • A V23 workflow was used to generate BBRI research report files in October 2026.
  • The project roadmap includes validation, audit, portfolio-risk, institutional-flow, and decision-engine modules.

Prototype and iterative research system. Data-source coverage, report completeness, and signal quality must be checked for each run; outputs are research support, not investment advice.
